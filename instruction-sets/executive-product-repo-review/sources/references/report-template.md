# Executive report format

## 1. Executive verdict
One paragraph: what this product is, apparent strategic promise, biggest MVP weakness, and bold recommended direction. Confidence level and access limitations.

## 2. Product thesis and target audience
- Core job-to-be-done, who benefits, buyer if distinct, currently observed experience, differentiation hypothesis.
- Specify what is **Observed**, **Inferred**, **Hypothesis** or **Unknown** and cite paths/issues.

## 3. Portfolio inventory and triage
| Capability | Role: core/differentiator/support/experimental | Evidence/status | Customer value | Disposition | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
Give strengths and structural gaps, not just an inventory.

## 4. User journey and retention diagnosis
| Journey stage | Current experience / evidence | Friction or risk | Recommended intervention | Validation signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
Stages: discovery, signup/onboarding, first win, repeated value, expansion/advocacy. Identify absent evidence rather than assert churn.

## 5. Strategy options
A. **Narrow winning MVP**: focused promise, user, necessary features, explicit omissions.
B. **Ambitious north star**: strategic advantage, adjacent opportunities, prerequisites and disqualifying conditions.
Optional C. alternative segment or business model when plausible.
State case **for** and **against** each.

## 6. Roadmap and decisions
| Horizon | Initiative | Customer outcome | Why now/later | Validation gate | What not to build |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
Include Now, Next, Later, and Remove/Freeze. Make dependencies clear.

## 7. Executive action memo
Three protect/invest bets, three defer/cut calls, biggest assumptions, business evidence still needed, and next review trigger. Suggest owner roles when useful, not fake named owners.

## Reporting standards
No coding fixes or detailed technical designs unless asked. Do not claim market positioning, retention or operational readiness without supporting evidence. Be concise, actionable, and willing to say 'this is a distraction.' Avoid exhaustive wish lists. Summarize actionable business decisions in the first screen.
