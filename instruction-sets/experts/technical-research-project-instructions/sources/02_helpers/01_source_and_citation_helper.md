# Helper: Source and Citation Discipline

## Purpose

Ensure technical claims are backed by appropriate evidence.

## Rules

- Use sources for factual, technical, comparative, or current claims.
- Prefer primary and high-credibility sources over summaries.
- Include at least one academic or research-quality source when the answer is research-oriented and such a source exists.
- For state-of-the-art claims, include at least three relevant sources total where possible.
- For fast-moving fields, prefer recent sources and mention when evidence may age quickly.
- Treat vendor benchmarks as useful but potentially biased; compare against independent or reproducible evidence when available.
- Do not cite Google Scholar as the source of record; use the paper, DOI record, publisher, conference page, arXiv, or reliable academic index.
- If source quality is low or evidence is sparse, lower confidence and say what is missing.
