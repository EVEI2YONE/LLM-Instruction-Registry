# Final Validation Checklist

Use this checklist before producing a final handoff package or asking the user to approve downstream execution.

## User approval checks

- Core idea and intended outcome are correct.
- In-scope and out-of-scope boundaries are explicit.
- Business risks and adoption risks are visible.
- Architectural decisions include rationale and tradeoffs.
- Technical constraints are listed.
- Development phases have dependencies and exit criteria.
- Validation strategy includes tests, artifact checks, or approval gates.
- Stack choices are explicit and approved.
- Alternative stack choices are documented.
- Modular structure is explicit and approved.
- Dependency direction rules are explicit.
- Schema, enum, and constant ownership are explicit.
- Research questions are clear and actionable.
- Research boundaries prevent silent architecture rewrites.
- Antigravity IDE guidance identifies what to reconcile against real code context.
- Claude guidance includes subagent/worktree decomposition rules.

## Artifact quality checks

- No major section is left implied.
- No platform decision is hidden in prose only.
- No modular rule is vague or unenforceable.
- No unresolved question is omitted when it could affect execution.
- Handoff artifacts are usable by downstream agents without additional explanation.
