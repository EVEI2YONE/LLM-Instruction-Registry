# Readiness deliverable acceptance and material finding preservation

This procedure applies when the user asks for a feature-readiness, release-readiness, QA, product scope or similar substantive assessment. **Successful spawning, instruction propagation and subagent completion do not complete the parent user request.** The requested report itself must pass an independent completeness check.

1. Collect the complete child or sequential specialist outputs before synthesis. Enumerate each **material** requirement, finding ID, status, impact, evidence reference, dependency, validation result, unresolved decision and limitation in a parent-owned findings ledger. Material includes release blockers, significant product gaps, failed/untested validation, requirements mismatches, and dependencies affecting readiness. Do not silently omit a material finding because its source is verbose. Deduplicate truly identical items but preserve all distinct evidence and impacts.
2. Prepare an accessible user-facing report: **verdict first**, then a concise requirement-to-feature status table, major gaps/blockers with impact and source references, passed/failed/untested validation, prioritized dependency-ordered completion backlog, limitations, and unresolved decisions. Explain what is not verified. Do not bury the product analysis beneath provenance, execution IDs or instruction receipts; keep orchestration records in a short methods note or appendix.
3. Before reporting COMPLETE, compare the report to the material findings ledger one by one. Every material finding must have a corresponding representation in the report with its impact and evidence, and each blocker/gap must map to actionable work. Distinguish blocked dependencies from independent tasks. Check that QA/test results retain their passed, failed, and untested distinctions; do not convert passing automation into production readiness.
4. If any material finding, evidence limitation, acceptance result, or prerequisite is omitted, the report is `READINESS_REPORT_INCOMPLETE`. Expand or revise the deliverable before closing. A short chat summary can accompany a full report, but cannot silently replace the requested substantive assessment.
5. `runtime/readiness_report_gate.py` provides a **deterministic structural regression checker** for normalized finding IDs, requirement coverage, impact/evidence identity, validation classes, and backlog dependencies. Its caller must actually inspect the specialist artifacts; a model-created ledger and report can both omit the same finding, so the test alone cannot prove real-world completeness.

For Little Wins: after receiving product-scope and QA specialists, retain all material findings across both, including onboarding, management workflow, review detail, staging acceptance, dependencies and validation limitations if supported by the actual reports. The list is an example from the reported incident, not a substitute for re-reading actual specialist artifacts.
