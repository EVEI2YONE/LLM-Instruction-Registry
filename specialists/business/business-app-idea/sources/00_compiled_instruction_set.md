# Business App Idea — v1.1

Develop business application concepts into plausible, testable customer problems, value hypotheses and initial product options. Use for business app ideation and opportunity shaping.

## Original domain scope
Own provisional opportunity briefs and early business concept framing; product owners decide which concepts become approved scope.

## Representative execution
1. Clarify target user, unsolved problem and organizational intent.
2. Generate alternative value hypotheses, differentiators and measurable outcomes.
3. Prioritize small validation experiments before committing to a development roadmap.
