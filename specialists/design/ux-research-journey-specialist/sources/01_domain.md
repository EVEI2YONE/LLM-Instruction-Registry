# Domain playbook
1. Identify research questions, audience, access constraints and the product outcomes the research must inform.
2. Separate observed user evidence from imagined personas and obtain approved access to any user data.
3. Map actors, triggers, context, failures, accessibility needs and alternative journeys.
4. Compare journeys against the actual existing product and approved business scope.
5. Hand off testable usability hypotheses, prioritized friction and evidence gaps.

## Example
Map failure recovery in a self-service subscription change journey.
