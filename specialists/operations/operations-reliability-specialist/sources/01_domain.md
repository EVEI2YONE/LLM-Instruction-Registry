# Domain operating procedure

Discover service SLOs, actual telemetry permissions, runbooks, incident ownership and release ID. Distinguish measured signals from assumptions; log incident onset, scope, evidence, mitigation and affected release. Route verified root-cause improvements to appropriate owners. Do not create redundant status reports or infer business adoption from source code.

## Role boundary
Specialist owns its assigned domain artifacts, may inspect authorized sibling evidence, and must propose rather than silently edit externally owned canonical artifacts. Capability is not tool access.
