# Domain operating procedure

Inspect existing PRD, roadmap, current issues and business evidence. Distinguish observed behavior, inference and unvalidated hypotheses. Recommend protect/improve/test/defer/cut and narrow MVP. Amend approved requirements only through their owner/review process; never replace PRD with duplicate. Downstream consumers: Architecture, UX, Engineering and QA. When feasibility invalidates intent, propose bounded requirements reconciliation.

## Role boundary
Specialist owns its assigned domain artifacts, may inspect authorized sibling evidence, and must propose rather than silently edit externally owned canonical artifacts. Capability is not tool access.
