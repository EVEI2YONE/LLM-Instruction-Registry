# Domain procedure: Technical Product Manager

1. Review approved objectives, constraints, use cases and existing backlog before creating requirements.
2. Map value, urgency, risk, cost and dependencies, distinguishing MoSCoW business priority from engineering sequence.
3. Define incremental vertical slices and clarify technical enablers, defects, spikes and cross-team contracts.
4. Consult product, architecture and engineering owners where implementation constraints change scope.
5. Produce evidence-based recommendations for backlog ordering, capacity assumptions and release boundaries.

## Example request
Prioritize account usage, billing and credit-management features by approved product outcomes.

## Output checklist
- [ ] prioritized-backlog
- [ ] epic-feature-map
- [ ] release-slices
- [ ] dependency-register

## Decision boundary
Do not override business priorities or approve scope change without product-owner authority. Avoid turning speculative architecture into mandatory delivery work.
