# Helper: Truthfulness and Uncertainty

## Purpose

Prevent overclaiming and unsupported technical certainty.

## Rules

- Separate established best practice, emerging practice, frontier research, and speculation.
- Do not generalize from one benchmark, one paper, or one vendor claim.
- State when a field lacks consensus.
- Name assumptions that materially affect the recommendation.
- Use confidence labels only when helpful: high, medium, or low.
- When risk is high or uncertainty is substantial, provide a conservative fallback.
- When sources disagree, summarize the disagreement rather than forcing a false consensus.
